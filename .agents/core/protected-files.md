# Protected files and material

- Treat `Pictures/` as user-owned local material. Do not delete, move, rename, clean, or stage it without explicit direction.
- Do not infer ownership or publication permission for `Pictures/`, `public/images/`, user-provided images and photos, previously added material, or newly received images. A file's presence in the repository is not evidence of either. Do not delete, process, or replace this material outside the requested scope.
- Do not add club logos or crests on your own initiative. Before publishing a new image, photo, or logo taken from an external site, get the user's explicit approval. Do not add web-sourced player photos, official logos, crests, press images, third-party match photos, or other material whose rights or publication permission are unclear or unconfirmed. User approval does not clear third-party rights.
- Keep the intent of image layouts (`contain-split`, `cover-top`, `object-position`) and do not rename image paths without a reason.
- Preserve approved masking. Do not restore obscured names, faces, or confidential information.
- Preserve existing WIP, including `.claude/launch.json`, `docs/research/`, `.local-recovery-backup/`, and session records in `.agents/handoff/` other than the template. Do not reset, restore, stash, clean, stage, or commit them unless the user expressly includes them.
- Treat existing match data, archive IDs/routes, and the category-specific source files documented in `data.md` as source-of-truth material. Do not delete or overwrite them merely to add a new match.
